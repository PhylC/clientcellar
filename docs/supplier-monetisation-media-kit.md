# ClientCellar supplier monetisation media kit

Updated: 2026-08-15

This document supports supplier-direct monetisation for ClientCellar. It is intentionally conservative: do not claim traffic numbers, partnerships, conversion rates or supplier outcomes unless current analytics prove them.

## Positioning

ClientCellar helps UK business buyers plan corporate wine gifts, client hampers, staff rewards and wine tasting events.

The site is useful for suppliers because visitors are usually not browsing casually. They are shaping a buying brief, checking supplier routes, planning budgets or preparing to contact merchants and gifting providers.

## Audience

- UK professionals planning client gifts, staff gifts, partner thank-yous or event drinks.
- Buyers who need a practical shortlist before contacting suppliers.
- People comparing corporate wine gifts, hampers, event wine suppliers or tasting providers.
- Users who may need supplier-ready enquiry copy, budget guidance and internal approval notes.

## Current commercial products

### Editorial supplier listing

No paid inclusion. Suppliers can be listed where they are useful, relevant and transparent for business buyers.

### Tracked or affiliate links

Used only where a genuine approved tracking or affiliate arrangement exists. Links should be placed only where they are useful to the buyer.

### Sponsored placement

Clearly labelled visibility on relevant supplier, guide or planner pages. Sponsored visibility should not override editorial usefulness or make unsupported claims.

### Supplier profile update

Suppliers can share corporate ordering details, brochures, service regions, minimum order notes, fulfilment strengths and contact routes for editorial review.

## Suggested sponsored placement structure

Keep the first commercial offer simple while traffic is still growing:

- Sponsored supplier card on a relevant supplier/category page.
- Clearly labelled "Sponsored" or "Sponsored supplier".
- Short supplier description reviewed by ClientCellar.
- Link to a relevant corporate gifting, event, hamper or trade page.
- Optional supplier contact email or enquiry route where appropriate.
- No fake ranking, fake review score or implied official partnership.

Avoid promising:

- guaranteed lead volume
- guaranteed sales
- live price/stock accuracy
- official recommendation status
- exclusivity unless separately agreed

## Media snapshot checklist

Before contacting suppliers, gather current evidence:

- Google Search Console clicks, impressions and top queries.
- Top organic landing pages.
- Supplier-directory page views.
- Supplier outbound clicks from analytics.
- Planner starts and completed free plans.
- Premium upgrade clicks and checkout starts.
- Device split, if useful for suppliers.
- Any relevant seasonal pages starting to rank.

Use ranges if exact numbers are sensitive, but do not inflate them.

## Internal pricing note

Do not publish a rate card until there is enough traffic and supplier response data to support one. Start with short test placements and direct discussion.
