# ClientCellar affiliate reapplication pack

Updated: 2026-08-15

Use this pack when reapplying to affiliate networks, supplier programmes or publisher review teams.

## Application summary

ClientCellar is a UK-focused business gifting and event drinks planning website. It helps professionals choose suitable corporate wine gifts, client hampers, staff rewards and wine tasting/event drinks suppliers using practical guides, free planning tools, supplier-directory pages and a paid Premium Brief Pack.

The site is not a voucher-code or discount site. It is a planning and supplier-discovery resource for buyers who are still deciding what type of supplier fits their brief.

## Suggested publisher description

ClientCellar helps UK business buyers plan corporate wine gifts, client hampers, staff rewards and wine tasting events. The site publishes practical buying guides, free planners, supplier-directory content and supplier-ready premium brief packs. Recommendations are editorially selected and based on buyer usefulness, budget fit, presentation, delivery practicality, corporate ordering support and suitability for professional relationships.

Commercial links are disclosed clearly. Affiliate potential does not guarantee inclusion or positive placement, and ClientCellar does not claim live prices, stock or supplier availability unless verified directly.

## Programme fit

Prioritise programmes where ClientCellar can send relevant buyer traffic:

- corporate wine gifts
- wine hampers
- premium food-and-drink hampers
- Champagne and sparkling gifts
- event wine suppliers
- wine tasting providers
- premium non-alcoholic gift suppliers
- business gifting providers with alcohol or hamper ranges

Avoid low-fit programmes where the only connection is generic retail alcohol, especially where corporate ordering, gift suitability or event planning is weak.
