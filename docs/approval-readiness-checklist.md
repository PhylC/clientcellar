# ClientCellar Approval Readiness Checklist

Internal checklist for Google indexing, affiliate network reapplication, and direct supplier review.

## Pages Reviewed

- Homepage
- Gift planner
- Event planner
- Guides index
- Representative guide detail pages
- Suppliers directory
- Pricing
- FAQ
- About
- Contact
- Terms
- Privacy Policy
- Affiliate Disclosure
- Editorial Policy
- Supplier Partnerships
- Network readiness profile
- Premium Brief Pack pages
- My packs recovery page

## Sitemap And Robots

- `/sitemap.xml` returns valid XML.
- Sitemap uses canonical `https://clientcellar.co.uk` URLs.
- Sitemap includes public canonical pages and guide URLs.
- Sitemap excludes admin, API, checkout, success, cancel, auth, and noindex pages.
- `/privacy-policy` is the canonical privacy URL in the sitemap.
- `/robots.txt` allows crawling and points to `https://clientcellar.co.uk/sitemap.xml`.

## Content And Commercial Standards

- No fake testimonials, fake reviews, fake partnerships, fake discounts, or fake traffic claims.
- No claims of live pricing, live stock, live availability, or confirmed supplier quotes.
- Supplier links and supplier references are presented as research or buying routes unless a relationship is explicitly labelled.
- Affiliate disclosure appears on guide and supplier pages where relevant.
- Alcohol content is framed as workplace-appropriate business planning, with responsible gifting and alcohol-free alternatives noted.

## Known Limitations

- ClientCellar provides planning guidance only and does not sell alcohol directly.
- Supplier pricing, stock, delivery, lead times and suitability must be confirmed directly.
- Premium Brief Packs use browser print/save, copy and text download actions rather than a dedicated PDF generator.
- Account login depends on deployment configuration.
- Supplier recommendations are editorial/commercial research routes, not guaranteed supplier availability.

## Manual Checks Before Reapplying

- Load the homepage, planners, pricing, guides, suppliers, contact, trust pages and one guide on mobile and desktop.
- Generate a gift plan and confirm supplier cards are visible in the free result.
- Generate an event plan and confirm supplier cards are visible in the free result.
- Confirm Premium checkout only appears after a free plan has been generated.
- Confirm `/sitemap.xml` opens in a browser and Search Console can fetch it.
- Confirm `/robots.txt` displays the canonical sitemap URL.
- Check footer links to trust pages and supplier partnerships.
- Check outbound supplier links open safely and do not imply fake partnerships.
- Review any newly added guide or supplier copy against the disclosure and brand-safety standards above.
