Responsible drinking

ClientCellar is a planning and supplier-direction resource for corporate gifts and event wine. We do not sell alcohol directly.

Legal drinking age and supplier terms

Alcohol should only be purchased and consumed by people of legal drinking age. Buyers should follow UK alcohol laws, supplier terms, delivery checks and any workplace rules that apply.

Corporate context matters

Alcohol is not appropriate for every recipient, client, employee, supplier, workplace or event. Treat alcohol gifting as a business decision, not a default.

Consider alcohol-free alternatives

Alcohol-free gifts, soft drinks, food hampers, gift cards or choice-based options may be more suitable in some situations, especially for employees and mixed groups. See our non-alcoholic client gifts guide.

Avoid inappropriate gifting

Do not gift alcohol where it may be unsuitable because of age, health, religion, culture, recovery, pregnancy, personal preference, workplace policy, occasion or local law.

Confirm company policies

Check your organisation's HR, procurement, expenses, anti-bribery and client entertainment policies before ordering gifts or events.

Plan events carefully

For tastings, keep pours modest, include food and water, provide alcohol-free options, avoid pressure to drink and make transport expectations clear.

Drink responsibly

If alcohol is included, it should be enjoyed responsibly by adults who choose to drink. Do not drink and drive.
