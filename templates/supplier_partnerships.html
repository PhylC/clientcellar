Supplier partnerships

ClientCellar helps UK businesses make better wine gifting and event drinks decisions. We are building an editorial directory of useful UK suppliers across corporate wine gifts, client hampers, event drinks and premium food-and-wine gifting.

Email supplier details Submit supplier View supplier directory

What we are looking for

  • UK delivery or UK service area
  • Useful corporate gifting, trade, events or hamper pages
  • Clear delivery/contact information
  • Good presentation and customer experience
  • Suitable options for business buyers

How listings work

  • Editorial listing where the supplier is useful for buyers
  • Affiliate link where available
  • Sponsored placement only if clearly labelled
  • No paid inclusion disguised as editorial recommendation

What we will not do

  • Fake reviews or ratings
  • Undisclosed paid placements
  • Claims of official partnership unless agreed
  • Claims of live prices, stock or quotes without reliable data

Commercial options

Ways suitable suppliers can work with ClientCellar

Commercial relationships are separate from editorial inclusion. A supplier must still be relevant and useful for business buyers.

Tracked or affiliate links

Where a supplier has an approved affiliate or tracked link, ClientCellar may use it on relevant supplier, guide or planner pages.

Sponsored placement

Clearly labelled sponsored visibility may be considered where the supplier fits the page and the buyer context.

Supplier profile updates

Suppliers can share current corporate ordering details, brochures, service regions and useful business-buyer information for review.
