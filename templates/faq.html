FAQ

Plain answers before you send a supplier email

Do you sell the wine?

No. ClientCellar is a planning tool. You buy directly from suppliers.

Are recommendations independent?

The MVP uses a curated planning database. Future affiliate links will be disclosed clearly.

Do you check live stock?

No. We do not check live stock, live prices, delivery slots or supplier availability.

Can you handle international delivery?

We can flag the issue, but alcohol shipping rules vary by country. Confirm customs, age verification, tax and courier rules with the supplier.

Do you support alcohol-free options?

Yes. Plans include reminders to offer alcohol-free alternatives, especially for employees and mixed groups.

Is this legal or procurement advice?

No. ClientCellar provides planning guidance only. Check your company policies and get professional advice where needed.

Do you work with suppliers?

Not yet. Supplier and affiliate partnerships can be added later with clear disclosure.

Can I use this for employee gifts?

Yes. The planner includes employee-friendly guidance, budget awareness and non-alcohol alternative prompts.
