Before you create a pack

Does this confirm supplier prices?

No. It structures the supplier conversation. Pricing, stock, delivery and availability must be confirmed directly.

Can I use it for events?

Yes. Event packs include a host brief, run-of-show, attendee template, inclusivity notes and supplier questions.

What happens after payment?

When Stripe is enabled and payment is verified, ClientCellar opens the paid pack view linked to the checkout token.

What if payments are disabled?

During MVP testing, payments can be disabled. In that case, use the register-interest route instead.
