Cookies

Cookie Policy

This policy explains how ClientCellar uses essential browser storage and how third-party supplier or tracked links may use cookies.

Essential cookies

Essential cookies may be used where needed to make the service work, keep forms secure or remember basic settings.

Browser storage

The site may use browser storage for practical features such as saved form state, clipboard actions, downloads or account/session handling where enabled.

Analytics and performance

If analytics tools are used, they should help us understand site performance and useful content, not identify sensitive recipient or gifting details.

Tracked supplier link cookies

If you click an outbound supplier or tracked link, third-party sites may set their own tracking cookies. Those cookies are controlled by the supplier, retailer or tracking service.

How to control cookies

You can usually block or delete cookies in your browser settings. Some website features may not work correctly if essential cookies are blocked.

Changes

We may update this policy when tools or tracking arrangements change.
