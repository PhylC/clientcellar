Cookies

Cookie Policy

This simple policy explains the likely cookie and storage approach for the ClientCellar MVP.

Essential cookies

Essential cookies may be used where needed to make the service work, keep forms secure or remember basic settings.

Anonymous session or local storage

The current front end may use browser features such as clipboard, downloads and page state. If anonymous session storage or local storage is added later, it should be described here.

Analytics placeholder

The MVP does not need analytics cookies to function. If analytics is added later, users should be told what is collected and why.

Affiliate tracking cookies

If you click an outbound supplier or affiliate link, third-party sites may set their own tracking cookies. Those cookies are controlled by the supplier, retailer or affiliate network.

How to control cookies

You can usually block or delete cookies in your browser settings. Some website features may not work correctly if essential cookies are blocked.

Changes

This page should be updated before commercial launch if cookie banners, analytics or advertising tools are introduced.
