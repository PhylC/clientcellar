About ClientCellar

ClientCellar helps UK businesses choose better wine gifts for clients, teams and corporate occasions.

ClientCellar was founded by Phyl, a commercial sales professional who understands how much business relationships matter — and how easy it is for corporate gifting to become rushed, generic or harder than it needs to be.

The idea is simple: help busy professionals find thoughtful wine gifts without spending hours comparing suppliers, second-guessing budgets or trying to work out what feels appropriate.

What ClientCellar focuses on

  • Client thank-you gifts
  • Employee and team rewards
  • Wine planning for corporate events

How recommendations work

Our recommendations are currently editorially selected. That means we look for UK suppliers and gift options that appear useful, credible and relevant for real business situations.

Over time, ClientCellar may use affiliate links or commercial partnerships. Where that happens, we will aim to keep recommendations useful, transparent and focused on helping buyers make better decisions.

UK business gifting, made clearer

We are UK-focused for now, with an emphasis on practical supplier options, clear gifting guidance and recommendations that feel appropriate for professional relationships.

ClientCellar does not sell alcohol directly, confirm supplier prices, guarantee stock or claim delivery availability. Buyers should confirm product details, pricing, VAT, delivery and suitability directly with each supplier before ordering.
