Important checks before ordering

  • Confirm current stock, pricing, VAT, delivery charges and lead times directly with the supplier.
  • Check age restrictions, recipient suitability, alcohol-free alternatives and workplace gifting policies.
  • Ask about substitutions, delivery reporting, invoice requirements and personalisation proofs before payment.
