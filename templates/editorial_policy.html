Editorial policy

Editorial policy

ClientCellar writes practical UK wine gifting and event drinks guidance for business buyers. The aim is to help people choose more thoughtfully, not to dress advertising up as advice.

How we choose suppliers

We look for UK suppliers with useful corporate gifting, wine gift, hamper, event drinks or business ordering pages. We consider buyer usefulness, presentation, delivery practicality, clear contact routes and suitability for business gifting.

A supplier can be included because it is useful editorially. Inclusion does not mean the supplier is a ClientCellar partner.

How we write gift and event advice

We write around the situation first: who the gift is for, why it is being sent, whether it needs to be shared, how sensitive the relationship is and what the buyer should check before ordering.

We avoid copied retailer descriptions and do not claim to have personally tested suppliers unless we can say that truthfully.

Affiliate links

Some links may be affiliate or tracked links where a genuine commercial arrangement exists. Affiliate potential does not guarantee inclusion, ranking or positive wording. Editorially useful suppliers can be listed without an affiliate relationship.

Read the affiliate disclosure for the plain-English version.

Sponsored placements

Sponsored placements will be labelled where they appear. We do not present paid inclusion as an editorial recommendation.

Corrections and updates

Supplier pages, delivery policies and gifting ranges change. If something is outdated or unclear, contact ClientCellar and we will review it.

Age and alcohol responsibility

ClientCellar does not sell alcohol directly. We link to third-party suppliers and retailers. Buyers are responsible for checking supplier terms, delivery availability, suitability and alcohol-related requirements before purchasing.

Alcohol is not suitable for every recipient or workplace. We encourage buyers to consider alcohol-free alternatives and check internal gift, procurement and hospitality policies where relevant.
