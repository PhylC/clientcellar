ClientCellar publisher profile

Commercial overview for supplier partners and review teams.

Site description

ClientCellar is a UK-focused corporate wine gifting and event planning website for people choosing client gifts, staff gifts, business hampers and wine for company events. We publish practical buying guides, planning tools and supplier-direction content to help business buyers choose suitable options by budget, occasion, recipient type and use case.

Audience

Our audience includes sales teams, account managers, office managers, founders, event organisers and small business owners. ClientCellar helps brands reach high-intent buyers before they have chosen a retailer or supplier.

Content categories

Corporate wine gifts, client gifts, staff gifts, wine hampers, Champagne and sparkling gifts, non-alcoholic alternatives, event wine planning, virtual tastings, buying etiquette and supplier brief preparation.

Promotional methods

We focus on useful editorial guidance rather than voucher-code or discount-led promotion. Recommendations are based on suitability, budget, presentation, delivery practicality and buyer usefulness.

Disclosure standards

We clearly disclose commercial relationships. Sponsored placements, if introduced in future, will be clearly labelled. Commercial relationships do not guarantee positive editorial placement.

Brand safety

ClientCellar does not target under-18s, does not encourage excessive drinking, does not publish fake reviews and does not claim live prices, stock or supplier availability unless explicitly verified.

Supplier suggestions and commercial partnership questions: partners@clientcellar.co.uk.
